# Lead-Gen Form Optimisation

**Prospects abandon the lead form**

> I started filling in the form but it asked too much or broke, so I stopped.

How might we make the lead form quick and painless enough to finish?

## You'll recognise this when

- Low form completion rate, drop-off on specific fields, high cost per lead from paid traffic.

## What this recipe does

Prospects start the lead form but abandon it before submitting, and the cost of every lost completion is high. Form analytics is the spine here, showing the exact fields where people stall, re-enter or quit, and session recordings add the behaviour around those fields. Analytics frames how many are affected, and an on-site poll asks what stopped them. A shortened or restructured form is then checked in usability sessions before launch, so the changes are grounded in where the friction genuinely sits.
