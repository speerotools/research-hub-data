# Lead Quality Research

**The pipeline fills with leads sales can't close**

> I downloaded the guide because it looked useful. I was never going to buy anything.

How might we attract fewer, better-fit leads instead of more of the wrong ones?

## You'll recognise this when

- Lead targets get hit while pipeline and revenue stay thin

- Sales rejects, recycles or quietly ignores a large share of handed-over leads

- Gated content fills the CRM with students, researchers and job-seekers

## What this recipe does

The lead count looks healthy and the pipeline doesn't, which usually means the acquisition engine is optimised for volume rather than fit. This recipe diagnoses the gap from both ends. CRM analysis separates the sources, campaigns and offers that produce closed-won customers from the ones that produce noise, and structured input from the whole sales team turns "the leads are junk" into specific, recurring rejection patterns. Interviews with recent well-fit buyers establish what a good lead actually looked like on the way in. Panel testing then checks that requalified messaging and offers attract the buyer and politely lose the browser.
