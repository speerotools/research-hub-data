# Product Page Optimisation

**Product views don't turn into add-to-carts**

> I looked at the product but couldn't get the answers I needed to commit, so I left it.

How might we give product pages the information and reassurance that earns the add-to-cart?

## You'll recognise this when

- High product-page traffic with low add-to-cart rate

- Repeated pre-sales questions

- Attention stopping above key content

## What this recipe does

People reach the product page but don't add to basket, and it's unclear whether the blocker is information, doubt or price. Analytics shows how product pages perform relative to each other and where attention stops, while recordings and heatmaps reveal which content people seek out, skip or expand. On-site polls and a short survey draw out the unanswered questions and hesitations that hold a purchase back. Usability sessions then confirm whether a revised page actually resolves them, so the changes address real doubt rather than guesswork.
