# Acquisition Research

**Paid visitors bounce off landing pages**

> I clicked the ad expecting one thing, the page doesn't match it, so I leave.

How might we make the landing page deliver on the promise the ad made?

## You'll recognise this when

- High ad spend with weak ROAS

- High landing-page bounce

- Poor message match between the ad and the page

## What this recipe does

Paid traffic is arriving but converting poorly, and it is unclear whether the problem sits in the ads, the landing pages, or the match between the two. This recipe connects acquisition spend to the on-site experience so you can improve ROAS rather than simply cut budget.
Ads analysis and analytics data analysis set the quantitative picture: which campaigns and channels underperform, and what those visitors do once they land. A UX heuristic review flags likely friction on the landing pages, and a 5 second test reveals whether the first impression and message match the promise made in the ad. The data shows where the leak is, the qualitative methods suggest why, and optimisation effort lands on the right page with the right message.
