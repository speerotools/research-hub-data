# Repeat Purchase & Post-Purchase Research

**First-time buyers never place a second order**

> The first order was fine. Nothing after it gave me a reason to order again.

How might we make the second order feel like the natural next step rather than a fresh decision?

## You'll recognise this when

- Revenue leans almost entirely on new-customer acquisition

- Repeat purchase rate stays flat while acquisition costs climb

- The post-purchase programme is an order confirmation and not much else

## What this recipe does

Most first-time buyers never come back, and the margin lives in the orders they never place. This recipe finds out why the second order isn't happening. Purchase cohort analysis shows the repeat rate, the typical gap to a second order, and which first products lead somewhere versus nowhere. The support team's structured input surfaces what buyers experience after checkout: delivery, returns, and the gap between the listing and the product in hand. One-time buyers explain why there was no second order, and repeat buyers explain what actually brought them back, which are rarely the same answer. Post-purchase and reorder concepts then get tested head to head with past buyers before the lifecycle programme is rebuilt around them.
