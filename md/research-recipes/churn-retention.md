# Churn & Retention Research

**We don't know why customers churn**

> I signed up but stopped getting enough value to justify keeping the subscription.

How might we understand and reduce the reasons customers leave?

## You'll recognise this when

- Rising churn, low repeat usage, cancellation reasons citing value or fit.

## What this recipe does

Customers cancel and the business is left guessing whether it was price, value, a missing feature or a bad experience. Start with the traces customers leave behind. Analytics shows where usage tails off before a cancellation, CRM and win-loss data show patterns in who leaves, and live chat logs surface the complaints that recur. A cancellation survey then quantifies the leading reasons, and interviews with churned customers explain the why behind the numbers. The layers turn a vague churn figure into specific, addressable causes.
