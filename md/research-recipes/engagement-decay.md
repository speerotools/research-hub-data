# Engagement Decay & Habit Research

**Activated customers drift away after the early weeks**

> I liked it at first. Then life got busy, I stopped opening it, and nothing gave me a reason to come back.

How might we help the product earn a place in customers' routines after the novelty fades?

## You'll recognise this when

- Activation numbers look healthy while week-four-plus retention slides

- A wide gap between weekly and monthly active customers

- Exit surveys say "just stopped using it" more than any specific complaint

## What this recipe does

These customers activated, found value, and then drifted; not because anything broke, but because the product never earned a place in the routine. Onboarding research won't catch this, because the loss happens after onboarding succeeds. Cohort analysis shows which behaviours separate the customers who stay from the ones who fade and when the fade starts. Diary studies then do what no analytics can: a week or two inside customers' real routines showing where the product fits, what competes with it, and what quietly replaces it. Interviews with drifted-but-recoverable customers explain what changed. Re-engagement and habit-hook concepts get prototype-tested before anything is built.
