# Agentic Commerce Readiness Audit

An audit of how well AI shopping assistants and agents can find, read and buy from a site. It checks product data, structured markup, policies and checkout readiness, then flags what stops an agent recommending or completing a purchase.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stage | Explore, Validate |
| Data type | Quantitative |
| Strength of signal | Low |
| Effort | Low |
| Surface area | Acquisition & ads, Checkout & cart |

## When to reach for it

Find out whether AI assistants can see accurate prices, stock, shipping and returns information.Spot gaps in structured product data that keep products out of AI recommendations.Set a baseline before fixes, then re-run it to track progress.

## Strengths

Free scanning tools make a first pass quick and cheap.
Issues found are usually concrete and fixable, such as missing schema or unclear policies.
Covers a fast-growing channel that most research methods ignore.

## Limits

Most readiness tools are static scanners, so a good score doesn't prove a real agent can complete a purchase.
AI assistants change often, so results date quickly.
It measures the site's readiness, not how customers actually use AI assistants to shop.

## Practical considerations

Practical considerations

### Pair it
Add an AI Agent Walkthrough of real checkout flows, and talk to customers who started their search in an AI assistant.

### Measure the channel
Track AI-referred visits and orders in analytics so fixes can be judged on real traffic.
