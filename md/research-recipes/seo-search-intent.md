# SEO & Search Intent Research

**The people searching for what we solve don't find us**

> I searched for exactly the thing they do. They weren't there, so I went with someone who was.

How might we show up for the searches our best customers are already making, in their words rather than ours?

## You'll recognise this when

- Organic traffic is concentrated on branded terms

- Competitors hold the rankings for the problem-language queries

- Content ranks for informational terms that never convert

## What this recipe does

The customers who most need what the business does are searching for it in their own words and finding someone else. This recipe closes the language gap between how customers describe the problem and how the site describes the solution. Search data shows which intents the site is absent from and which rankings never earn the click, interviews surface the vocabulary customers actually use, and a review of the winning results pages shows what earning each intent takes. A glance test on the landing experience then confirms the click pays off before content investment scales. Search intent match is message match's organic twin.
