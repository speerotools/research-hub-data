# Mobile Experience Optimisation

**Mobile converts far worse than desktop**

> On my phone the site is fiddly, so I give up and mean to come back later, but I don't.

How might we close the gap between mobile and desktop conversion?

## You'll recognise this when

- Mobile conversion well below desktop, high mobile share of traffic, mis-tap and zoom behaviour in recordings.

## What this recipe does

Mobile traffic is large but converts well below desktop, and the team can't tell whether it's layout, speed, forms or intent. Begin by isolating the mobile segment in analytics to size the gap and locate it, then watch mobile session recordings and heatmaps to see the cramped taps, mis-hit buttons and scrolling that numbers alone hide. Form analytics exposes where mobile input breaks down, and an on-site poll catches frustrations in the moment. Validate fixes in usability sessions run on real devices, not a shrunken desktop window.
