# Trust & Credibility Audit

**Visitors hesitate because they don't trust us yet**

> It looks interesting but I'm not sure I can trust them with my money or my details.

How might we give visitors enough confidence to commit?

## You'll recognise this when

- High traffic with low conversion on commitment steps, weak or missing proof elements, low brand familiarity.

## What this recipe does

Visitors are interested but hesitate to commit because the site hasn't earned their confidence yet. A heuristic review flags the credibility gaps an expert eye catches quickly, such as thin proof, weak security cues or vague claims, and a competitor review shows the trust signals buyers see elsewhere in the category. Interviews, a survey and on-site polls then locate the specific doubts that stall real customers, and copy testing checks whether reassurance language actually lands. Together they separate the trust problems you can feel from the ones customers act on.
