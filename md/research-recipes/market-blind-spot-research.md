# Market Blind Spot Research

**We don't know how the market sees us**

> I've heard the name, but I couldn't tell you what they do differently or whether they're for someone like me.

How might we know how our market actually perceives us, and whether that perception is moving?

## You'll recognise this when

- A rebrand or repositioning is being scoped with no baseline of current awareness or perception

- Positioning debates inside the company run on opinion because nobody has market evidence

- Sales keeps correcting the same wrong assumptions about what the company does

- The brand is missing from consideration sets it should be in

## What this recipe does

Everyone in the category sells the same way, and nobody on the team can say what the market is missing or which angle is worth betting on. This recipe reads the whole category, not just the company: competitor sites and pricing, what incumbents tell investors, and what customers say unprompted in reviews and forums.
The analysis asks three questions. What do the leaders know that their customers would never admit? Which beliefs does the whole category take for granted, and could any already be false? Which pains keep coming up that nobody is building for? One angle is then attacked over several rounds, each bringing new evidence, until what is left holds up. The result is a sharp go-to-market hypothesis, and it goes to the buyers it describes before anyone builds on it.

## The method sequence

Explore. Focus. Validate.

### Explore
Read the category in its own words and in its customers' words.

- [Online Review Analysis](/research-methods/online-review-analysis)

### Focus
Find the shared blind spots, then attack one angle with new evidence each round.

- [Market Thesis Stress Test](/research-methods/market-thesis-stress-test)

### Validate
Put the surviving angle in front of the buyers it describes.

- [Copy Testing](/research-methods/copy-testing)
