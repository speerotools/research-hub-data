# Content-to-Lead Conversion Research

**Organic visitors read the content and leave**

> The article was useful. I read it, got what I needed and left. Nothing made a next step feel worth it.

How might we turn content readers into subscribers and leads without wrecking the reading experience?

## You'll recognise this when

- Content traffic grows steadily while lead volume stays flat

- Strong rankings and high entrances on posts with near-zero CTA engagement

- The newsletter and gated offers barely grow despite real readership

## What this recipe does

The content programme is doing its job of attracting readers, and then the relationship ends at the last paragraph. This recipe finds where the reader-to-lead path breaks. Analytics shows which posts attract visitors with any buying intent and where readers exit, heatmaps show whether the calls to action are even seen on the templates readers actually use, and on-site polls ask readers directly what they came for and what would make a next step worthwhile. Testing offer and CTA framings on a panel, then checking they register at a glance, de-risks the fix before it ships across hundreds of posts.
