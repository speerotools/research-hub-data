# Ads Analysis

Analysis of ads data within Google Ads, Facebook ads, Instagram ads, etc, focused on performance, CPC, Impressions, Clicks, Cost, with the aim of identifying which campaigns and ads are underperforming.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stage | Focus |
| Data type | Quantitative |
| Strength of signal | High |
| Effort | Medium |
| Surface area | Acquisition & ads, Landing pages |

## When to reach for it

Increase your ROI from acquisition spendImprove the effectiveness of paid landing pagesStart optimising acquisition activitiesConnect your acquisition strategy to your on-site optimisation efforts

## Strengths

Analysing your ads data helps you to optimise spend by highlighting which campaigns are performing well, and which are not.

## Limits

Ads analysis on its own lacks qualitative insight needed to really determine why certain efforts are not performing.

## Practical considerations

Practical considerations

### Expertise
Ideally, you need someone with acquisition knowledge to conduct this activity, at the same time, you want someone who isn't directly involved in your acquisition strategy, to prevent bias.
