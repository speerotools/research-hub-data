# Agentic Commerce Readiness

**AI shopping agents can't find or buy from us**

> I asked an assistant to find me one. It never suggested you, or it couldn't tell me the price.

How might we make our products easy for the AI assistants our customers shop through to read, recommend and buy?

## You'll recognise this when

- AI assistants recommend competitors for queries we should win

- AI-referred traffic is growing but rarely converts

- Assistants quote wrong prices, stock or shipping details for our products

- Product data and policies live in images or scripts that agents can't read

## What this recipe does

More shoppers now start in an AI assistant, and if the assistant can't read a store's products, prices or policies, it recommends someone else. This recipe checks how AI agents experience the site and how customers actually use them.
An agentic commerce readiness audit flags missing product data, unreadable policies and checkout blockers, and search and SERP analysis shows where the brand is absent from the questions customers ask. In Focus, an AI agent walkthrough of real checkout flows tests whether an agent can actually complete a purchase, and customer interviews with buyers who started in an AI assistant explain what they asked and why they chose where they bought.
To validate, re-run the audit after fixes and use analytics to track AI-referred visits and orders over time. A readiness score alone doesn't prove agents can buy, so the analytics trend is the measure that counts.
