# Search & SERP Analysis

Analysis of organic search performance and the surrounding results landscape. The queries that bring visitors in and the ones that should but don't, rankings and click-through against the pages that hold them, and the content competitors use to win the intents that matter. Pairs the site's search data with the language customers actually type. Branded query volume belongs here too, since demand for the brand by name, tracked over time, is one of the few behavioural reads on brand awareness.

## When to reach for it

Grow qualified organic traffic, close the gap between customer search language and site language, prioritise content investment by intent, and connect organic acquisition to on-site optimisation. Track branded search demand as a behavioural indicator of brand awareness, before and after brand-level changes.

## Strengths

Real behavioural data at scale from actual customer queries. Shows demand and competitive position without spending on a panel or media.

## Limits

Intent has to be inferred from queries rather than asked. Rankings move slowly, so validating changes takes time and can be confounded by algorithm updates.
