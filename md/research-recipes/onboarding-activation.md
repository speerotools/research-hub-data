# Onboarding & Activation Research

**New users don't reach first value**

> I signed up but couldn't quickly see the point, so I never really got started.

How might we get new users to their first win before they lose interest?

## You'll recognise this when

- High signup-to-activation drop-off

- Low day-7 return rate

- Early support tickets about getting started

## What this recipe does

New users sign up but never reach the first moment of real value, so they drift away before the product can prove itself. Analytics maps the activation funnel and the step where new users stall, and session recordings show what they try and abandon in those early minutes. Interviews and a survey explain what people expected and where reality fell short, while diary studies capture the experience across the first days rather than a single visit. Usability sessions on the onboarding flow then confirm whether a revised path gets people to value faster.
