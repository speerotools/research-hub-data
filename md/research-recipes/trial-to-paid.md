# Free Trial to Paid Conversion

**Trial users don't convert to paid**

> I tried it during the trial but never hit the moment that made paying feel worth it.

How might we make the value obvious enough during the trial that paying feels like the natural next step?

## You'll recognise this when

- Low trial-to-paid rate, trial usage tailing off mid-trial, upgrade prompts ignored, discount requests clustering at trial end.

## What this recipe does

People sign up for the trial but never convert to a paid plan, and the team can't tell whether the product failed to prove its value or the upgrade moment failed to land. Analytics maps what trial users actually do, separating those who convert from those who drift, and session recordings show the early sessions where the difference takes shape. Win-loss analysis of the CRM adds the pattern across accounts that upgraded and accounts that lapsed. An end-of-trial survey quantifies the leading reasons for not converting, and interviews with expired trials explain the gap between what people expected and what they experienced. Together these separate a value problem from a pricing or timing problem, which point to very different fixes.
