# SaaS Pricing Page

**Buyers can't tell which plan fits them**

> I can't work out which plan is right for me or what I'd actually end up paying, so I stall.

How might we help buyers self-select the right plan with confidence?

## You'll recognise this when

- High exit on the pricing page

- Low trial-to-paid

- Sales repeatedly answering the same plan-comparison questions

## What this recipe does

The pricing page is where SaaS buyers decide whether the product is worth it, and it is often where they stall. The goal is to understand why visitors hesitate, what they need in order to feel confident, and whether the plans, copy and layout match how they actually evaluate value.
Start broad with a competitor review and a UX heuristic review to see how rivals frame pricing and to surface obvious friction on your own page. Sales and customer service interviews bring in the real objections and questions buyers raise, which often explain hesitation the page never addresses. Copy testing checks whether plan names, feature descriptions and value framing are understood by the target audience. Remote usability studies then show how people move through the page and where they get stuck choosing a plan. The combination moves from market context, to internal knowledge, to message clarity, to observed behaviour, so no single lens carries the decision.
