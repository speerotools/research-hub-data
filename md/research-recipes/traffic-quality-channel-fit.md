# Traffic Quality & Channel Fit Research

**We're attracting the wrong visitors**

> The ad pulled me in but the offer wasn't what I actually wanted, so I left.

How might we attract visitors who match our best customers?

## You'll recognise this when

- High traffic with low qualified-lead rate, poor fit between ad audience and ICP, weak engagement from paid channels.

## What this recipe does

Traffic is growing but the visitors arriving aren't the ones the business is built for, and everything downstream pays for it. This recipe works out where the misfit traffic enters and what it costs. Behavioural data shows which sources send visitors who bounce, stall or never return, CRM data shows which sources actually produce customers, the ads layer shows which campaigns and queries are buying the mismatch, and the sales floor tells you what a wrong-fit visitor sounds like. Panel message testing then checks that sharpened targeting and positioning reach the right people before more media spend follows the fix.

Explore. Focus. Validate.
