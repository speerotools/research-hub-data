# Form Analytics

Form analytics is similar to regular analytics analysis, but focused on your website forms.  You can analyse metrics such as form field interaction, drop out rates, error rates, etc to understand which fields are causing friction.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stage | Focus, Explore |
| Data type | Quantitative |
| Strength of signal | High |
| Effort | Medium |
| Surface area | Forms, Checkout & cart |

## When to reach for it

Exploratory research has identified a specific barrier in your checkout or on other onsite forms.

## Strengths

Provides in-depth data to inform form optimisation; e.g. removing fields, changing layouts etc.

## Limits

Can be subject to technical issues, much like standard analytics tools, the accuracy of the configuration is integral.
